AC types we handle

From a single bedroom split to a full commercial floor

Whatever is on your wall, in your ceiling or on the roof, we sell it, install it and keep it running.

Split AC

The everyday choice for bedrooms, living rooms and small offices. Inverter and non-inverter, 0.8 to 2 ton.

Window AC

Compact, budget-friendly cooling for single rooms and rented flats. Quick to install and easy to service.

Cassette AC

Sits flush in the ceiling and throws air in four directions. Ideal for offices, clinics and restaurants.

Ductable AC

Hidden above the false ceiling with only the grilles visible. Suits showrooms, banquet halls and open offices.

VRV / VRF Systems

One outdoor unit serving many indoor zones, each with its own control. For commercial buildings and large homes.

Commercial HVAC

Air handling units, chillers, ducting and controls for offices, retail, hospitals and industrial spaces.
